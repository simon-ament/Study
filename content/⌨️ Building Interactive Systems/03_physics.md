# Branches
## Dynamics
branch of applied mathematics concerned with ​the study of **forces** and their effect on **motion**
- for example: [[06_hapkit|Hapkit]], paper planes (no quasi-infinite powers)

## Statics
just about **forces** (in equilibrium) but without motion
- for example: bridges and buildings

## Kinematics
just about **motion** but ignoring forces
- model of animal running $\Rightarrow$ rather than using forces it uses **quasi-infinite** power

# Drive mechanisms
## Slack / Backlash
ability of the driven gear to move without applying a force to the driving gear​
- **gears** couple two rotating elements, allowing the transmission of torque
- gears are subject to slack

## Friction drive
mechanism that converts rotary motion​ into linear motion and vice versa
- it is **slack-free**
- only works reliably if (rubberized and) **pushed together with a high force​**
- thus requires a **massive spring** to push rollers together​
- thus requires **ball bearings** $\Rightarrow$ reasonably big tech​

## Capstan and bowstring
mechanism that converts rotary motion into linear motion and vice versa​
- capstan = a reel or pulley (a wheel with a grooved rim) around which the bowstring is wound several turns​
- traction of capstan increases exponentially with the number of turns
